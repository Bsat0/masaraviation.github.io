How much should I budget for pilot training?

One illustrative calculation, step by step.

Short answerTuition is only part of it. Add rent and daily living for every month of training, add extras, then a contingency for delays. Keep any refundable deposit separate, as it is cash you need up front.

A worked example

These are sample numbers to show the method. They are not prices for any country or school.

  • Tuition: 60,000
  • Duration: 18 months
  • Rent: 600 per month
  • Daily living: 20 per day (30 days per month)
  • Extras: 2,500
  • Contingency: 10%
  • Refundable deposit: 1,200

Step by step

  1. Monthly living = 600 + 30 × 20 = 1,200
  2. Living for 18 months = 18 × 1,200 = 21,600
  3. Base expenses = 60,000 + 21,600 + 2,500 = 84,100
  4. Expense budget = 84,100 × 1.10 = 92,510
  5. Cash needed = 92,510 + 1,200 = 93,710

What if training takes longer?

Three extra months add 3,600 before contingency and 3,960 after it. At 24 months the expense budget becomes 100,430, an increase of 7,920.

What should I ask before choosing a programme?

Use this checklist with any school. Get the answers in writing.

Short answerAsk exactly what the programme leads to, what the price includes and excludes, what happens if training takes longer, and what the refund conditions are. Get the answers in writing.

The outcome

  • Which licence, ratings and exams will I hold at the end, and which authority issues them?
  • Is this an integrated or modular programme?

The price

  • What exactly is included: flight hours, simulator, exams, equipment, accommodation?
  • What is not included, and what does it usually cost?
  • How much does each extra flight hour cost?

Time and risk

  • What is the typical duration, and what usually causes delays?
  • What happens if I need more hours than planned?
  • What are the refund and cancellation conditions?

Life during training

  • Where will I live, and how far is it from the airfield?
  • What visa or residence permit do I need?

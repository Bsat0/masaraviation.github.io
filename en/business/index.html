For aviation businesses

Reach motivated pilot students in MENA.

For flight schools, airlines and aviation companies exploring the Middle East and North Africa, MASAR offers local understanding and a professional, well-informed link to prospective students.

What we can help with

Four ways to work together.

Each starts with a conversation about your objective.

01

Market development

Objective

Understand demand and student expectations in MENA markets.

Possible engagement

Market insight and introductions based on our work with students in the region.

Next discussion

Which markets and programmes you want to prioritise.

02

Student recruitment

Objective

Connect with well-informed prospective students who fit your programmes.

Possible engagement

Students who come to you having understood the route, the costs and your terms.

Next discussion

Your entry requirements, intakes and capacity.

03

Partnership development

Objective

Build a structured, long-term relationship in the region.

Possible engagement

An agreed way of working, with clear responsibilities on both sides.

Next discussion

What a successful partnership looks like for you.

04

Training coordination

Objective

Help students arrive prepared and stay on track.

Possible engagement

Coordination around preparation, timelines and communication with students.

Next discussion

Where students most often need support today.

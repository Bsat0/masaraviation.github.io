About Masar Aviation Group

A trusted bridge between ambition and the right training.

Choosing where and how to train is one of the biggest decisions an aspiring pilot makes. We help students and families across the Middle East, GCC, North Africa and South Asia understand their options before they commit.

For flight schools, we provide a professional, well-informed link to motivated prospective students.

We give honest guidance. We do not promise outcomes, jobs or approvals.

Our services

How we support your journey.

01

Pilot Training Advisory

Understand the training options in Europe and the USA, and the route that fits your goals.

02

Programme Comparison

Compare programmes by outcome, pace, what is included and the full cost, not just tuition.

03

Licensing Pathway Guidance

Understand licences, ratings and which authority’s rules apply where you want to work.

04

Training Preparation

Plan the steps before your start date: medical, documents, budget and the written terms.

05

Support Through Graduation

Formal support continues until you graduate, and you can still reach our team afterwards.

06

Free Consultation

30 minutes with the MASAR team, in Arabic or English, before you commit financially.
